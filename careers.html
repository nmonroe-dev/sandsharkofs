ROLE OVERVIEW

Field coordination positions support the movement and organization of frac sand operations. Work may involve truck communication, arrival sequencing, staging, site logistics, documentation, equipment coordination and communication with the teams operating around the pad.

RESPONSIBILITIES EXECUTION SEQUENCE
01
MOVEMENT Truck arrivals

Coordinate truck arrivals and field movement.

02

Communicate with dispatchers, carriers, field personnel and customer representatives.

Support staging, traffic flow and site organization.

04

Track operational information and communicate changes.

05
